One screen for the whole family's day.

Kindo is a self-hosted family dashboard for the kitchen wall, phones and desktops. Glance at it and see what's happening today and what each person still has to do. A four-year-old can follow their routine through pictures alone.

Built around the family's day

Not another smart-home panel: Kindo starts with the people and what their day looks like.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family lanes

One column per person, in their colour: the current routine as picture tiles, appointments and chores. School and Kita appear as a quiet line.

🧸

Child view

Large pictogram cards, progress as dots, morning, afternoon and evening picked by icon. Text is optional; pictures carry the meaning.

🖼️

Wall display

A kiosk layout readable from a few metres. Idle, it becomes a photo frame; a tap returns to the dashboard.

📅

Calendar

Month, week and agenda, colour-coded by person. Nextcloud/CalDAV, Google Calendar, ICS subscriptions and Kindo's own.

🔁

Routines & chores

Daily, selected days, every N weeks, monthly, school days. A daily reset, holidays from your state's feed, two weeks of history.

⭐

Calm rewards

Off, stars, tokens or pocket money, optional per chore, with parent approval if you like. No excessive gamification.

🛒

Everyday lists

Shopping lists that work offline, tasks, a weekly meal plan and important dates with countdowns.

🏠

Home Assistant

Presence for the photo frame, a few switches and the solar flow, Frigate cameras with the doorbell, and talking to Assist.

🎧

Kids' shelf

Music from Jellyfin and audiobooks from Audiobookshelf, played on the screen or a Home Assistant speaker.

Made for children who can't read yet

Lena's morning routine is a row of big picture cards. She taps one when she's done; a dot fills, and when the routine is finished the evening sky appears. Leaving the view needs a press-and-hold, so little fingers stay where they belong.

  • Large targets
  • Calm feedback
  • Pictures first
  • Points only if you want them

Everyone's week, one calendar

Every appointment carries its person's colour. Filter by person or calendar, and map each Nextcloud, Google or ICS calendar to the people it belongs to. Light, dark or follow the system.

  • Nextcloud / CalDAV
  • Google Calendar
  • ICS
  • English & German

On every screen

Yours, on your own server

Kindo runs with Docker Compose next to your other self-hosted services. Your family's data stays in your PostgreSQL; integrations run on the server and their secrets never reach the browser. Open source under the AGPL-3.0.

Setup guide Try the demo 
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MacNite/kindo/main/docker-compose.demo.yml
docker compose -f docker-compose.demo.yml up -d
# → http://localhost:3000
